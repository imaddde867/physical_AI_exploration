# Rectangular display and MCU boards

## Which 3–4 integrated boards are the strongest fit for a restrained peeking face?

### Takeaway
For the wired first build, prefer the LILYGO T-Display-AMOLED-Lite if its supplied official STEP is confirmed to match the board revision being bought: it integrates USB-C, charging, display, touch, and MCU in a compact face format, and vendor CAD can support connector-clearance design. Keep Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.64 as the easier-to-scale alternate because it has a documented overall envelope. Keep LILYGO T-Display-S3-AMOLED as a wider-face alternative. No candidate has been validated for physical lid mounting or display quality.

### Cited Findings
- **LILYGO T-Display-AMOLED-Lite, wiki revision V1.0**: 1.47-inch AMOLED, 194 × 368 pixels, nominal 450 nits, active area 17.46 × 33.12 mm (manufacturer store listing); ESP32-S3R8, 16 MB flash, 8 MB OPI PSRAM, QSPI display, CHSC5816 touch, AXP2101 PMU/battery charging, 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi and BLE 5.0, USB-C. Official docs say it lacks hardware screen rotation and list board buttons and display GPIO mapping. [Manufacturer wiki](https://wiki.lilygo.cc/products/t-display-series/t-display-amoled-lite/)
- LILYGO makes a PCB DWG, a PCB 3D model and a schematic available from its official repository. [LILYGO documentation/repository](https://wiki.lilygo.cc/products/t-display-series/t-display-amoled-lite/), [DWG](https://github.com/Xinyuan-LilyGO/LilyGo-AMOLED-Series/blob/master/dimensions/T-Display-AMOLED-Lite.DWG), [3D model archive](https://github.com/Xinyuan-LilyGO/LilyGo-AMOLED-Series/blob/master/dimensions/T-Display-AMOLED-Lite-PCB-3D.7z), [schematic](https://github.com/Xinyuan-LILYGO/LilyGo-AMOLED-Series/blob/master/schematic/T-Display_AMOLED-Lite.pdf)
- Official LILYGO listings conflict on AMOLED driver identifier: wiki says SH8501B0, store product specification says RM67162. Resolution and stated active-area values agree. Preserve this discrepancy and verify board revision/controller against schematic or silkscreen before coding the display driver. [LILYGO wiki](https://wiki.lilygo.cc/products/t-display-series/t-display-amoled-lite/), [LILYGO store specifications](https://lilygo.cc/products/t-display-amoled-lite)
- LILYGO’s own store lists $35.66 but marked the item sold out when checked; no Finland delivered price could be confirmed. [LILYGO store](https://lilygo.cc/products/t-display-amoled-lite)
- **Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.64, product SKU 31197**, currently listed as V2 at the vendor: 1.64 in AMOLED, 280 × 456, 350 cd/m² nominal, CO5300 over QSPI; ESP32-S3R8 with 16 MB flash and 8 MB PSRAM; FT3168 touch, QMI8658 IMU, TF slot, USB-C, 3.7 V MX1.25 battery charge/discharge connector, onboard antenna and Wi-Fi/BLE 5. [Waveshare SKU/product page](https://www.waveshare.com/esp32-s3-touch-amoled-1.64.htm), [wiki](https://www.waveshare.com/wiki/ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.64)
- Waveshare product page identifies SKU 31197 and currently names the item V2; the official resource page lists V1/V1.1 examples and schematics plus a generic Mechanical Drawing. There is also an official `waveshareteam/ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.64-v2` repository, but its sole schematic is explicitly named `ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.64-Rev1.1.pdf`. This suggests the schematic may be associated with the V2 repository, but does not definitively establish a `Rev2.0` PCB. Waveshare documentation for this board does not expose a version-identification/difference note. Keep V1.1 and retail V2 claims separate; match any purchased board by PCB marking before relying on pinout. [Waveshare product page](https://www.waveshare.com/esp32-s3-touch-amoled-1.64.htm), [official Resources and Documents](https://docs.waveshare.com/ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.64/Resources-And-Documents), [official V2-labelled repo](https://github.com/waveshareteam/ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.64-v2/tree/main/schematic)
- Waveshare’s official resource page links `ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.64-DAD.zip`; I downloaded it to `sources/` and inspected its 2024-12-21 primary outline PDF. It confirms 29.12 × 44.00 mm outer face, PCB 28.60 × 43.50 mm, 22.00 × 38.50 mm hole pitch, and viewing/active area 22.34 ±0.15 × 36.06 ±0.15 mm. Side view shows 9.60 mm overall projection for the illustrated assembly. The PDF has no visible V2 or PCB revision marking; therefore these are primary outline dimensions, but V2-to-drawing correspondence remains unverified. [Waveshare official resource index](https://docs.waveshare.com/ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.64/Resources-And-Documents), [Waveshare product page](https://www.waveshare.com/esp32-s3-touch-amoled-1.64.htm)
- Waveshare’s official price was $23.99 pre-shipping; OpenELAB Finland storefront showed €25.95 incl. VAT and “in stock/ready to ship” at research time; final shipping and Finland delivery timing are not included. [Waveshare store](https://www.waveshare.com/esp32-s3-touch-amoled-1.64.htm), [OpenELAB Finland listing](https://openelab.io/fi/products/waveshare-esp32-s3-amoled-touch)
- **LILYGO T-Display-S3-AMOLED, V1.0**, portrait face mounted landscape or native wide-face: 1.91 in AMOLED, 536 × 240 pixels, RM67162 QSPI, 350 nits; optional touch version; ESP32-S3R8, 16 MB flash, 8 MB OPI PSRAM, Wi-Fi/BLE 5, USB-C, two programmable buttons, LiPo charging support. Native USB D+/D− use GPIO20/19 and the official instructions use USB CDC; UART pins GPIO43/44 are also present. V1 vs V2 display power-enable differs: V2 GPIO38 enables display; V1 GPIO38 is a green LED. [LILYGO wiki](https://wiki.lilygo.cc/products/t-display-series/t-display-s3-amoled/), [official board pinout/resources](https://github.com/Xinyuan-LilyGO/LilyGo-AMOLED-Series)
- The T-Display-S3-AMOLED’s official repo supplies schematic, DWG, PCB STP and shell STP. [LILYGO resource index](https://github.com/Xinyuan-LILYGO/LilyGo-AMOLED-Series)
- I considered a Waveshare round AMOLED, but excluded it from the main shortlist because the priority changed to a rounded rectangle; its product listing is 466 × 466 at 500 cd/m², but round silhouette is weaker to the revised brief. [Waveshare 1.32 board specs](https://www.waveshare.com/product/esp32-s3-touch-amoled-1.32.htm)
- **Nominal, not independently measured:** all stated brightnesses, resolution and functional specs above are manufacturer claims. No actual board was tested for color, reflections, glare, readability at desk distance, refresh behavior, or measured brightness. No vendor specifies an achieved frame rate; do not infer one from QSPI. AMOLED burns/image retention can occur under prolonged static high-stress patterns; the actual risk on these modules is not quantified in the available board documentation. Mitigations for this always-on face are screen blank/dim when idle, restrained brightness, occasional subtle pixel-shift/face variation, and avoid persistent high-contrast status bars. [EBU advice on image retention](https://tech.ebu.ch/publications/r129)

### Inferences
- The AMOLED-Lite is physically most aligned with a petite face window while leaving room for rounded corners and short labels; its 17.46 × 33.12 mm active area is vendor-listed. A compact portrait panel will naturally make occasional text narrow.
- The Waveshare 1.64 is the easiest first concept to scale confidently because vendor-derived outline and mounting dimensions are published; it is still a finished 29 × 44 mm module behind a 28.6 × 43.5 mm board, not a flush glass-only panel. A polished housing should mask the CNC/bezel edge with a designed black aperture and soft corner radius.
- LILYGO T-Display-S3-AMOLED’s 1.91-inch wide panel is an option if eye expression needs more horizontal room, but its panel face and board CAD are materially wider than the first two. Turn the module 90° only if the manufacturer/display driver supports software rotation acceptably; verify with the board’s example before choosing portrait layout.
- The cited USB D+/D− native path means a direct USB CDC serial link to the Mac is plausible without an external UART bridge; host-side USB CDC recognition and firmware protocol work still need to be implemented and tested. Wi-Fi/BLE exist but are not necessary for the initial tethered prototype.
- The repo’s checked-in firmware targets the existing GC9A01/240 × 240 display and does not support any of these boards. The board changes require a display driver, pin map and UI port, plus a Mac bridge that the existing repo has only for the existing target.
- Apple lists the complete 14-inch MacBook Pro thickness as 15.5 mm; lid thickness is not established by that figure; a board mounting beside or behind the display glass must avoid the hinge and panel/glass stack, and must be tested on the actual laptop. Do not design a thin clamp from board dimensions alone. [Apple technical specifications](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/121552)

### Gaps
- Exact height/thickness and component protrusions (USB-C, switches, battery header, screen bezel) remain unknown from the parsed vendor dimension data for shortlisted parts. Manufacturer CAD exists for LilyGO AMOLED Lite and for the 1.91-inch LilyGO board; inspect a locally opened CAD before finalizing a case. Waveshare’s mechanical drawing is linked by distributor; obtain the actual V2 drawing and align it with PCB revision before fixing mounts.
- No reliable Finland stocked retailer for LilyGO AMOLED-Lite was found. Official price is stale/unavailable in checkout because listing was sold out. OpenELAB Finland advertised the Waveshare board with an EU stock indicator, but shipping to Finland was not quoted.
- Waveshare uses native USB hardware on ESP32-S3, but exact USB-C wiring/CDC behavior should be checked against current V2 schematic; no board-specific native serial enumeration trial was performed.
- No ambient-light usability or outdoor-reading evidence. 350–450 nits are nominal manufacturer ratings and do not establish perceived readability under reflections; for an indoor peeking face, OLED contrast helps in dim rooms, while glossy cover reflection can dominate in bright rooms.
- No product-specific OLED static-image lifetime or burn-in warranty/rating was located. The EBU source supports the general persistence risk, not a failure time for these particular panels.

## What drawing, electrical, and integration evidence exists?

### Takeaway
Prefer boards with manufacturer pin maps and downloadable mechanical resources. The best documented current path is LILYGO AMOLED Lite for compactness and the Waveshare 1.64 for traceable envelope dimensions, but revision labels and mechanical data need to be reconciled before ordering. Do not assume the existing project firmware supports them.

### Cited Findings
- Waveshare reports Type-C for power, firmware download and debug, USB/UART/I2C peripherals, QSPI display and I2C capacitive touch; 2 × 11-pin 2.54 mm headers are included as package accessories, not necessarily installed. In the Waveshare-team repository labeled `...1.64-v2`, its `Rev1.1` schematic shows USB-C U_N/D’_N routed through 22 Ω resistors to ESP32 GPIO19 and U_P/D’_P through 22 Ω to GPIO20. This is primary evidence for that schematic revision; because the schematic title is Rev1.1 rather than Rev2.0, it does not establish the wiring on every board sold as V2. USB CDC examples also document use of USB CDC when flashing/printing. The downloaded PDF is `sources/ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.64-Rev1.1.pdf` (SHA-256 `70345ca6e1b5385bcc362fa74ac8692a735648b8a4afa3377e2cc8599a03c14b`). [Waveshare wiki](https://www.waveshare.com/wiki/ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.64), [official product/package](https://www.waveshare.com/esp32-s3-touch-amoled-1.64.htm), [official repository schematic](https://github.com/waveshareteam/ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.64-v2/blob/main/schematic/ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.64-Rev1.1.pdf)
- LILYGO AMOLED Lite’s official mapping reserves GPIO 7/10/11/12/5/4/40/6 for display data/clock/chip-select/reset/TE, GPIO 1/2 for touch and PMU I²C, GPIO0 and GPIO21 buttons, GPIO19/20 native USB, and GPIO43/44 UART. This is useful for a Mac USB serial bridge, but mapping and USB CDC must be checked against the delivered revision. [LILYGO wiki pin diagram](https://wiki.lilygo.cc/products/t-display-series/t-display-amoled-lite/)
- LILYGO T-Display-S3-AMOLED supports QSPI-based RM67162 through the official library. The T-Display-S3-AMOLED Plus is a different board: same nominal 1.91-inch size/resolution and 350-nit rating, but official repo pinout lists SPI display and additional PMU/RTC/SD hardware. Do not conflate it with base AMOLED. [LILYGO series product matrix](https://github.com/Xinyuan-LilyGO/LilyGo-AMOLED-Series), [official pin tables](https://github.com/Xinyuan-LilyGO/LilyGo-AMOLED-Series)
- The current repository’s hardware notes identify its present target as 240 × 240 GC9A01 and firmware draws that target. This is repository-local evidence; it is not manufacturer support for one of these new boards. [Existing purchase audit](../../reports/MacDesk%20Buddy%20purchase%20audit.md), [project firmware](../../firmware/src/main.cpp)
- The `step.parts` API was queried using the installed bundled search script for exact Waveshare 1.64, LILYGO AMOLED Lite, and LILYGO T-Display-S3-AMOLED names. API responded successfully with zero candidates for each. This is a catalog search miss, not proof no CAD exists; manufacturer CAD links above are stronger evidence. [step.parts API](https://api.step.parts/v1/parts)

### Inferences
- For a wired first assembly, no battery or wireless behavior is needed; power from USB-C and use USB CDC JSONL or a similar small command protocol. LILYGO documents USB CDC on boot and USB-C programming/debug; actual macOS enumeration still needs testing. Battery charging capability is convenient hardware, not a reason to include a LiPo in the first build.
- More PSRAM and resolution support richer animation buffers but do not make the screen a Mac monitor; these remain MCU-rendered display devices.

### Gaps
- No board was acquired, flashed, or enumerated over macOS USB; no cross-platform firmware port was run.
- The repo’s existing circuit and firmware remain tied to the original display. Board-specific font/layout sizing, display coordinates, screen rotation, touch input, and USB serial protocol are open implementation work.

## What should the first concept assume before purchase?

### Takeaway
Use the LILYGO AMOLED Lite as the first integrated candidate for the wired prototype, subject to CAD/revision confirmation. For an immediately scaled numeric reference, the Waveshare 1.64 has the clearest published outer dimensions: 29.12 × 44.00 mm. Make the design a visually restrained rounded bezel/short stand mount rather than a permanent adhesive lid attachment until actual Mac fit and hinge sweep are checked.

### Cited Findings
- Waveshare’s primary outline PDF gives 29.12 × 44.00 mm overall, 28.60 × 43.50 mm PCB, 22.34 ±0.15 × 36.06 ±0.15 mm viewing area, 22.00 × 38.50 mm hole pitch, and 9.60 mm overall side projection for the illustrated assembly. It does not identify hardware revision, so confirm the bought board matches. [Official mechanical resources](https://docs.waveshare.com/ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.64/Resources-And-Documents)
- LILYGO explicitly offers PCB drawings and 3D model archives for AMOLED Lite. [LILYGO wiki resources](https://wiki.lilygo.cc/products/t-display-series/t-display-amoled-lite/)
- Downloaded official Waveshare mechanical archive in `sources/`: `ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.64-DAD.zip` SHA-256 `aa7e59e13789193d7640d580154f8a67f4f5377a05b42b97fa6a740bc0409526`; extracted outline `Waveshare-ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.64-mechanical-drawing-20241221.pdf` SHA-256 `94cbc14dbc566165785c81b8b48cb963e7360f59b16696fe23b26392ab5bdb0c`. It also includes official STEP model, not inspected here for revision correspondence.
- Downloaded official CAD in `sources/`: `T-Display-AMOLED-Lite-PCB-3D.7z` SHA-256 `9bf2122663c0a3ed0c1cdb7d3664b5f7d918b307d876030135dec4df508b46de`; extracted integrated `t-display-amoled-lite.stp` SHA-256 `4beafd20ef37e069bcc63191fdc9483a66ce7671be5efb081ba0e409315af990`; `T-Display-AMOLED-Lite.DWG` SHA-256 `0aba9f3b60c53bc2cf0ac797b8b24cd9b3b8ea12c029668944d5ee689d114787`. Upstream is [LILYGO’s MIT-licensed repository](https://github.com/Xinyuan-LILYGO/LilyGo-AMOLED-Series). Parent CAD inspection found the integrated STEP contains four leaves: PCB 25.7 × 52.2 × 2.0 mm, TP 19.36 × 37.67 × 0.78 mm, PMMA 25.7 × 45 × 5.8 mm plus a duplicate PMMA node. The modeled native envelope is 25.7 × 52.2 × 7.0 mm. This is a verified CAD leaf/envelope measurement, but the model appears incomplete (it lacks separate USB/MCU/electronic components and duplicates PMMA) and is not explicitly linked to the currently documented board revision, so it cannot establish the complete physical or USB-plug envelope.
- User’s host documentation records a 2024 14-inch M4 MacBook Pro body thickness as 15.5 mm and describes the existing concept as an unverified desk shell rather than an edge mount. [Host profile](../../docs/development-host.md), [Apple specs](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/121552)

### Inferences
- Preliminary Lite display aperture: active W×H = 17.46 × 33.12 mm per LILYGO store specification. CAD leaf envelope is 25.7 × 52.2 × 7.0 mm, as measured by the parent; treat as incomplete-board model dimensions, not a complete assembly. Alternative Waveshare 1.64 outer envelope is 29.12 × 44.00 mm; its official drawing gives a 22.34 ±0.15 × 36.06 ±0.15 mm active/viewing area, 9.60 mm side projection, and 22 × 38.5 mm hole spacing. Drawing-to-V2 correspondence remains unknown.
- Do not incorporate the included 2.54 mm pin headers into a thin enclosure; leave headers unsoldered unless breakouts are required. Budget cable exit and USB-C side access from the CAD and real board, since USB-C plug strain relief may be more cumbersome than the PCB thickness.
- Face first: keep a default face state, short opt-in labels only, dim/blank idle to limit burn-in and avoid distracting a Mac user. Brightness and legibility should be judged at desk viewing angle in dim and lit rooms before settling on OLED versus LCD.

### Gaps
- No final wall, mount or display viewing angle can be recommended before the assembled unit is tested against the actual MacBook lid and hinge motion. No claim of harmless attachment to the glass, screen, or coating has been established.
- The Lite STEP leaf dimensions above were measured by the parent, but CAD-to-revision correspondence and completeness remain open. Waveshare V2 physical thickness/component height and USB-C insertion/strain-relief clearance are unknown. Verify full board parts, orientation, mounting and cable envelope before final enclosure dimensions.
