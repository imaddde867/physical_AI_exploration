# Rectangular MacDesk Buddy build plan

**Direction.** Build a small, matte, rounded-rectangle face as a locally rendered USB accessory. Keep it powered and connected by one discreet USB cable. Use the Mac as the event source and the ESP32 as the display renderer. Keep the first version deterministic and local; the hardware foundation should remain useful without a model, hosted inference, camera or recurring token cost. Select the Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.64 SKU 31197 V2 as the first bench-test purchase, checking the delivered board revision against the official V2 examples before flashing or finalizing its enclosure. The [2026-10-29 decision](build-decision.md) is the current buying and feature-scope record. LILYGO T-Display-AMOLED-Lite is a fallback candidate, not an automatic purchase: it was listed sold out, its driver name conflicts across official pages, and its available older STEP does not model the complete board.

## Staged work

**First prototype decision (2026-10-27).** Fixed landscape orientation on the upper right side of the lid. Following the user's annotated photo, lower the head so its upper edge is approximately 25 mm below the lid top, and angle its face inward toward the user by a provisional 10° about its vertical axis. Build the angle into the fixed arm; no adjustable hinge, swivel, sliding deployment, or automatic orientation is needed. The head/arm assembly must detach for backpack transport, leaving a low-profile rear dock. Retain the provisional 48 × 33.12 × 13 mm housing and aim for a narrow side gap after checking the angled housing's full clearance. The new `selected_placement.py` study shows this angled placement with a 2 mm side gap; the older comparison image still shows the earlier parallel face and 8 mm top offset. These are design targets, not measurements from the supplied photos or validated mounting clearances. The photos show the front of the laptop without a ruler; they cannot establish rear attachment geometry, lid thickness or a safe adhesive interface.

1. **Buy one documented board for bench testing.** Compare total delivered prices to Finland for the exact Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.64, SKU 31197, using published sources. No seller contact is required. Official documentation identifies native USB for power, programming and logging. Check the received PCB revision against the corresponding examples before flashing; measure the actual board before final enclosure CAD. Treat advertised stock, taxes and shipping as unverified until shown for the actual order. Do not buy a battery or optional sensors.
2. **Measure and bench the board.** Record revision, X/Y/Z, active window, USB connector and plug envelope, buttons, headers, holes, mass, and cable exit. Power from the Mac through the intended cable; confirm macOS enumerates a native USB CDC serial device, send/receive a bounded command, replug, and inspect peak/average current and warmth. Check readability, reflections, brightness, color and rotation at desk distance. Capture the board, firmware, cable, OS, and results.
3. **Implement a minimal renderer path.** Port only the selected display driver and pins, draw the face locally, and add short optional text plus allowlisted state commands to the existing Mac-to-device path. Keep animation and typography on the ESP32; do not stream frames. Acceptance goals—not promises—are 30 fps local animation, p95 command-to-visible update under 100 ms, 10,000 clean state updates, and reconnect within 3 seconds without reboot. Record measured outcomes and revise goals if the panel cannot meet them.
4. **Measure Mac and mount interface.** On the exact 14-inch base M4 MacBook Pro (Mac16,1), record lid edge thickness, local curvature, corner radius, camera/notch/logo positions, display boundary, hinge sweep, closed gap, and cable clearances. Use a nonpowered gauge and paper outline first. Develop the selected detachable head/arm and rear dock around the measured attachment surface and storage envelope. Never infer lid geometry from Apple's 15.5 mm whole-computer thickness.
5. **Print a fit gauge before a functional shell.** Use the H2S installed nozzle and matching Bambu Studio profile. The current coupon is one strip with three holes at 0.2/0.4/0.6 mm total dimensional clearance plus a separate 4 × 8 mm peg on a foot. It checks fit only; it is not a latch, detachable interface, retention or strength test. Print and inspect it with the actual material profile, then measure the result. Do not put electronics in unvalidated clamps or rely on adhesion to bare laptop finish.
6. **Integrate and accept.** Make the measured board shell removable from the mount. Verify lid travel, camera/screen visibility, strain relief, closed-lid/bag envelope, desk handling, sustained operation, reconnect and repeated attach/detach cycles. Only then prepare a functional print and record the exact revision, slicer profile, material, dimensions and test results.

## Conditional first buy and consumables

- **One display/MCU board:** Waveshare SKU 31197 V2 for the first bench prototype; verify revision and mechanical fit on receipt. Its $23.99 vendor price and a Finland storefront listing are reference prices only; shipping, final stock and Finland landed total were not verified.
- **One short USB data cable:** select after confirming board connector orientation and Mac port clearance; it must carry USB data and power. Buy only after confirming cable length, right-angle orientation if needed, and bend/strain relief route.
- **Printing consumables:** small amount of PLA and PETG only if not already available, matched to the H2S nozzle/profile. Use sacrificial backing for later surface/interface trials. No new printer, model, battery, camera, IMU, touch accessory, external UART adapter, or cloud service is required for the first build.

**Stop conditions:** no flashing with a mismatched revision example; no final CAD before physical board and Mac measurements; no travel-ready or finish-safe claim before repeated mechanical and surface tests.
