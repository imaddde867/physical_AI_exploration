# Build a rectangular MacDesk Buddy carefully

> Decision update, 2026-10-29: [the first build decision](../docs/build-decision.md) supersedes the purchasing advice below. Select one Waveshare 1.64 V2 for bench testing, then verify the received board before flashing or fitting. This dated report preserves earlier research.

Start with a **matte, rounded-rectangle face**, one short USB cable for both power and data, and a small locally rendered display module. Treat the Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.64 (SKU 31197, listed V2) as the leading documented purchase candidate only after its delivered revision, mechanical drawing, native USB CDC path, and Finland shipping are confirmed. Keep the LILYGO T-Display-AMOLED-Lite as a compact alternative, not a default buy: it is listed sold out, its official sources disagree on the display driver, and its older CAD does not contain the full electronic assembly. First concepts can establish appearance and scale, but neither Apple’s published specifications nor board drawings establish a safe lid fit. This is a wired prototype plan, not a travel-ready mount or measured performance claim.

## A documented rectangle is the best first scaling target

The Waveshare 1.64 combines a display and ESP32-S3 on one USB-C board. Its vendor listing identifies SKU 31197 as V2 and lists a 280 × 456 AMOLED, CO5300 QSPI display, nominal 350 cd/m², ESP32-S3R8, 16 MB flash and 8 MB PSRAM, plus touch, IMU, microSD, Wi-Fi/BLE and a battery connector. Only the display, MCU, and USB port matter to the first build; touch and other onboard capabilities can remain unused. Its broad portrait proportions suit the requested softened-rectangle design and short status text. The brightness and panel specifications are seller claims, not a comparison measured on a desk. ([Waveshare product page](https://www.waveshare.com/esp32-s3-touch-amoled-1.64.htm), [Waveshare wiki](https://www.waveshare.com/wiki/ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.64))

The official Waveshare DAD drawing gives **29.12 × 44.00 mm outer face**, 28.60 × 43.50 mm PCB, 22.34 ±0.15 × 36.06 ±0.15 mm viewing area, 22 × 38.5 mm hole pitch, and a 9.60 mm dimensioned side projection. This raises the dimensional evidence above distributor transcription, but the drawing has no visible V2 or PCB revision marking, so its match to a retail V2 board remains unverified. The official Resources and Documents page provides the drawing, and a V2-labelled repository contains a Rev1.1 schematic. That schematic routes native USB D+/D− on GPIO19/20; current V2 revision mapping is still unknown. Confirm board marking before using either mechanical or electrical details as final. These sources still do not validate physical fit or USB enumeration on the Mac. ([Waveshare official resources](https://docs.waveshare.com/ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.64/Resources-And-Documents), [Rev1.1 schematic](https://github.com/waveshareteam/ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.64-v2/blob/main/schematic/ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.64-Rev1.1.pdf))

LILYGO’s T-Display-AMOLED-Lite is smaller and portrait-oriented: its official sources list 194 × 368 pixels, 17.46 × 33.12 mm active area, ESP32-S3, USB-C, touch and battery charging. LILYGO publishes a schematic, PCB drawing and 3D model archive, but those resources do not remove the need to match the model to the purchasable revision. The wiki names the driver SH8501B0 while the store lists RM67162; verify the chip and board silkscreen before firmware work. LILYGO’s listing was sold out at research time and showed $35.66, without a confirmed Finland delivered price. These conflicts make it a gated fallback, not a ready substitution. ([LILYGO wiki and resources](https://wiki.lilygo.cc/products/t-display-series/t-display-amoled-lite/), [LILYGO store](https://lilygo.cc/products/t-display-amoled-lite))

Local CAD inspection of the supplied Lite STEP measured a modeled overall envelope of **25.7 × 52.2 × 7.0 mm**. Its four leaves include a 25.7 × 52.2 × 2.0 mm PCB, 19.36 × 37.67 × 0.78 mm TP, and two identical 25.7 × 45 × 5.8 mm PMMA nodes. It omits separate USB and MCU components, duplicates the PMMA, and has no established match to the current purchasable revision. This is useful evidence about the model’s contents, not a complete physical board or connector envelope. Do not use the earlier raw STEP point bounds as dimensions.

A third candidate, LILYGO T-Display-S3-AMOLED, offers a wider 536 × 240 panel and official schematics and CAD. It is a reasonable alternative if a wider expression face wins after paper mockups, but its separate V1/V2 display-enable behavior and optional touch variation require exact revision selection. It should not be confused with the different “Plus” model. No selected board has been tried for text readability, glare, color, viewing angle, frame rate, or mount fit. AMOLED contrast may help indoors, but reflections can dominate; dim or blank the face when idle and avoid a static bright status bar. ([LILYGO board documentation](https://wiki.lilygo.cc/products/t-display-series/t-display-s3-amoled/), [official resource repository](https://github.com/Xinyuan-LILYGO/LilyGo-AMOLED-Series), [EBU image-retention guidance](https://tech.ebu.ch/publications/r129))

## Keep USB commands small and render the face on-device

ESP32-S3 provides USB-OTG Full-Speed and USB-Serial-JTAG paths. Espressif lists Full-Speed at a nominal 12 Mbps bus rate and says USB-Serial-JTAG works on macOS without a separately installed driver; that does not prove the selected board wires its connector to the desired controller or firmware exposes CDC. The Waveshare V2 schematic and real board must confirm native USB data and power on the same connector. The Lite source documents USB CDC on boot, but its revision and driver issues remain open. Do not assume a USB connector means native CDC, and do not assume a UART bridge is present. ([Espressif USB overview](https://docs.espressif.com/projects/esp-iot-solution/en/latest/usb/usb_overview/usb_overview.html), [USB-Serial-JTAG guide](https://docs.espressif.com/projects/esp-iot-solution/en/release-v2.0/usb/usb_overview/usb_serial_jtag.html))

The existing project’s firmware targets a 240 × 240 GC9A01 display and draws images locally; it does not support the rectangular options. Its Mac demo already sends compact JSONL face commands over a serial-style path, but the selected board needs a new display driver, pin map, UI sizing and Mac-side device integration. Preserve that simple division: send allowlisted state changes and brief text, then render animation and typography locally. Sending images continuously couples the host to the panel format and wastes the link. For scale, a hypothetical 280 × 456 RGB565 framebuffer is 255,360 bytes (about 249 KiB) per frame, or **61.2864 Mbps at 30 fps**, before framing or retransmission. Even the Lite’s 194 × 368 RGB565 frame is 142,784 bytes, or 34.26816 Mbps at 30 fps. Both exceed USB Full-Speed’s nominal 12 Mbps before overhead. The demo’s 115200 setting is a configured serial parameter, not the USB CDC bus capacity. ([project architecture](../docs/architecture.md), [demo](../macdesk/demo.py), [firmware](../firmware/src/main.cpp), [Espressif USB rate](https://docs.espressif.com/projects/esp-iot-solution/en/release-v2.0/usb/usb_overview/usb_otg.html))

Set **30 fps local animation** and **p95 command-to-visible-state below 100 ms** as initial targets, not achievements. Measure panel refresh, host-to-visible latency, sustained current and reconnect behavior on the selected assembly. Test 10,000 state updates and aim for USB reconnect within three seconds without a board reboot; record failures and update targets based on actual hardware. BLE and Wi-Fi can wait. BLE adds pairing, permission and recharge work; Wi-Fi adds setup and local-network permission paths that do not benefit this event-driven face. The first cable must be a verified data-and-power cable routed with slack and strain relief, away from the hinge sweep and pinch points. ([ESP32-S3 datasheet](https://documentation.espressif.com/esp32_s3_datasheet_en.pdf), [Apple local network privacy](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/technotes/tn3179-understanding-local-network-privacy), [Apple Bluetooth usage description](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/BundleResources/Information-Property-List/NSBluetoothAlwaysUsageDescription))

## Model appearance now; defer the functional mount until measured

Three right-side form studies compare portrait 33.12 × 48 mm, landscape 48 × 33.12 mm, and a 35 × 35 mm square. The rectangle inputs start with the Waveshare outer module size of 29.12 × 44 mm plus a nominal 2 mm rim per side; they are not the active viewing area or a finished aperture. The square is illustrative, area-matched and has no selected board. The local concept models use a **13 mm assumed depth**, a packaging-study envelope around the available vendor CAD rather than a finished enclosure. The vendor STEP contains 263 leaves and spans 29.12 × 44.2 × 13.9 mm with its two PIN_HEADER leaves included; excluding only those leaves leaves 261 modeled leaves with a 29.12 × 44.2 × 9.6 mm bounding box. Keeping headers excluded gives a provisional package depth near the drawing’s 9.60 mm projection, but clearances, display centering, aperture, cable approach and revision match remain unchecked. A separate 13 mm case study is modeled around this vendor geometry with placeholder window/cable placement. Its STEP contains the 261-leaf board model, shell and cover; no positive-volume overlap was found across 522 board-to-shell/cover pairs. The estimated cavity-envelope margins are 0.5 mm at the sides and vertically, 0.1 mm at the front and 0.7 mm at the rear. The closest-point query was inconsistent, so these are not exact minimum surface clearances. ([packaging rebuild guide](../cad/peeking-concepts/README.md), [source](../cad/peeking-concepts/src/packaging_study.py)) The rectangular STEP study passed its saved-geometry check: 42 valid positive-volume solids, matching placeholder screen areas, 2 mm edge gaps and a rear-only envelope. This verifies model geometry only. The separate packaging study remains provisional, not fit-ready CAD or a physical-fit result. The square remains a visual concept, not a buyable board.

No official lid CAD or dimensioned lid drawing for the exact 14-inch base M4 MacBook Pro (Mac16,1) was located in the bounded research. Apple publishes 312.6 × 221.2 mm body dimensions and 15.5 mm whole-computer thickness; that 15.5 mm is not the lid thickness. Apple’s repair manual and accessory resources do not provide lid mating geometry. The prior 214 mm lid height and 4.5 mm lid thickness are placeholders. No fit-ready claim can derive from them. ([Apple technical specifications](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/121552), [Apple repair manual](https://support.apple.com/en-gb/120922), [Apple accessory resources](https://developer.apple.com/accessories/))

Before designing a functional mount, measure the exact laptop at the intended location: lid edge thickness and local rear curvature/flatness; local corner radius; distances to top and side edges, camera/notch and Apple logo; display glass/bezel boundary; hinge and rubber cover location; ports and cable path; available closed-lid gap; and the full hinge sweep. Also record Mac finish, case/skin presence, and where a removable interface could sit. A nonpowered outline and gauge should first verify clearance with the laptop open and closed. Prefer a replaceable case or sacrificial skin for the load path; test peel, slip and residue on representative material. Do not squeeze the lid, load display glass or trust a bare-aluminum magnet. Retract versus detach remains a product choice: decide after measuring bag envelope, arm travel, snag exposure and the acceptability of carrying a removable part. Apple does not certify added hinge torque, adhesive safety or an allowable accessory mass. ([iFixit model reference](https://www.ifixit.com/Device/MacBook_Pro_14%22_Late_2024_M4), [Apple product information](https://cdsassets.apple.com/live/6GJYWVAV/information/locale/es-mx/macbook-pro-14inch-m4-2024-info.pdf))

## Print coupons before a shell or arm

Use the confirmed Bambu Lab H2S with the installed nozzle and matching Bambu Studio profile. Bambu’s upstream profile set includes an H2S 0.4 mm nozzle machine, a 0.20 mm Standard process, and PLA Basic/PETG HF presets. These are available profiles, not proof of the locally selected slicer configuration. The current fit coupon is one assembly: a strip with three holes and a separate 4 × 8 mm test peg on a foot. The hole sizes provide 0.2, 0.4 and 0.6 mm total dimensional clearance variants. It is a fit gauge only, not a detachable interface, retention or strength test. Its STL passed geometry checks as two watertight bodies, with 45 × 27.5 × 8 mm overall bounds and 2,076.1 mm³ volume; sampled minimum wall was 2 mm, and the analysis found zero unsupported down-facing area at the 45° threshold. Slicer and physical print behavior remain unverified. Use the matching spool profile; neither material name nor printer profile certifies strength or creep life. ([H2S machine profile](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/master/resources/profiles/BBL/machine/Bambu%20Lab%20H2S%200.4%20nozzle.json), [H2S process profile](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/bambulab/BambuStudio/master/resources/profiles/BBL/process/0.20mm%20Standard%20%40BBL%20H2S.json), [PETG guide](https://help.prusa3d.com/article/petg_2059))

For the current peg-fit coupon, check only peg fit in each of its three holes; it does not assess board fit, retention, removal force or cable routing. After measuring the actual board and Mac, make separate board-fit, retention and cable-interface coupons. Check those later coupons for connector access, removal force, slip, cable strain and surface marking on sacrificial material, then hand-cycle a dummy-mass joint and verify lid travel, camera field, display visibility, port access and the closed-lid/bag envelope. Only after those checks should the measured electronics go into a printed shell. PETG is a sensible first indoor functional material; PLA is for appearance and quick fit. Heat, layer orientation, print settings, arm leverage and local geometry change real strength, so do not assign a load rating from generic filament guidance. ([Prusa material comparison](https://help.prusa3d.com/filament-material-guide), [Bambu H2S specifications](https://bambulab.com/it/support/buying-guide))
