# Mission: Waveshare RP2040-Touch-LCD-1.69 for MacDesk Buddy

## Why
First hardware/IoT project: make the ordered Waveshare board show the animated companion character, driven from the Mac over USB, and understand every step well enough to debug it alone.

## Success looks like
- Explain what each chip on the board does and which bus it uses.
- Flash MicroPython, run the vendor demo, then our blink, and read the results correctly.
- Predict whether a drawing approach fits in RAM and SPI bandwidth before trying it.
- Send one command from the Mac over USB serial and see the display react.

## Constraints
- Board delivered and flashed 2026-11-12 (ordered 2026-11-04, ~EUR 30 budget); record bench results before moving past the concept lessons.
- Short lessons; no walls of text.

## Out of scope
- Final enclosure, magnets, contacts, battery, Wi-Fi.
