What to record

MicroPython version, real SPI rate, free heap after the display is created, filesystem size, I2C addresses, and a note on any tearing or flicker. These replace the open items in docs/hardware/bringup-log.md.

Safety basics

Logic on this board is 3.3 V. The USB data/power cable is enough for everything above. Do not connect a lithium battery until you have read the wiki's battery section; I have not read it.

Check

No RPI-RP2 drive appears. First suspect?

The bootloader is in ROM and ignores your code. A cable without data wires is the usual cause.

Why run the vendor demo before ours?

If the vendor code works and ours doesn't, the fault is in our code, not the hardware.

Which step answers the flash-size question?

os.statvfs reports the filesystem MicroPython can use.
